Core intent insight by user

Help the runner train properly to the race plan and adapt it based on (1) how the runner feels and (2) race destination and route conditions.

Most running apps cheer you on. This one gets you to the finish line, injury-free. positioning line by coach
Root need · from Five Whys

Runners lack a personal, honest early-warning voice calibrated to their own body and thresholds. Apps cheer, nobody warns. The runner is the only sensor, so the app must learn their personal baseline, and both reassure (novice false alarms) and warn (veteran grit).

Mary's connection insights

01

The body map is the hub

One input powers injury warning, Smart Skip, good/bad pain, Recovery Lap and the doctor report. Build its data model well in sprint 1.

insight by coach

02

Same principle at both levels

The runner decides in the app (Why? cards), and the Vibe CEO decides in the build (BMAD decision docs). The product and the LinkedIn story carry one trust message.

insight by coach

03

"Everyone is different" ×3

Pain, plans, fuel. Thresholds must use personal baselines, not population averages. The architect should design for calibration from day one.

insight by coach

04

The route gap, resolved

"Route/destination conditions" is in the core intent, but Course Twin and climate gap sat in COULD. Resolution: a lightweight race profile (manual total elevation gain + expected race-day temperature) promoted to sprint 2.
